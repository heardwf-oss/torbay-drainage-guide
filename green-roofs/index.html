Where it fits

Buildings where the structure, waterproofing and access can support the system.

What to consider

Roof loading, waterproofing, drainage layers, rainfall performance, safe access, fire considerations, plant choice and overflow.

Design limits

Roof loading and plant needs limit the suitable system. A green roof does not normally replace all downstream drainage.

Maintenance

Inspect outlets, vegetation, growing medium and waterproofing access points.

Evidence to check

A green roof draining only its own surface has a defined interception assumption in national standard 2.7. Show how excess runoff reaches the next feature.

Check the full drainage requirements and lifetime maintenance arrangements for the proposed system.

Detailed component design: CIRIA C753, chapter 12. Read alongside the current national standards below.

Relevant assessment checks

ER-06Green roofs and wallsConditional

Surfaces with green roofs or walls that drain only their own area comply.

Applies when
Green roofs or walls.
What to check
That no other area drains onto the roof.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

This is a compliance presumption for the proposed feature, not a requirement to provide it. Apply the agreement and calculation conditions in ER-05 and national standards 2.6.2–2.7.

CM-12Design life and loadingRequirement

Show components last the development's design life (or include replacement in the plan) and resist design loads with factors of safety. Underground storage must suit the final surface use.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Product specifications; structural adequacy of crates under parking.
Evidence to look for
Asset-specific maintenance plan and inspection information; Ownership, adoption and usable access arrangements; Construction, phasing, commissioning and structural evidence
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.
