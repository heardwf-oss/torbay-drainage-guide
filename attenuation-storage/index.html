Where it fits

Sites needing temporary storage after reuse, infiltration and other measures have been assessed.

What to consider

Required volume, levels, inflows, outlet control, blockage protection, structural loads, access and exceedance.

Design limits

Storage alone does not deliver all seven standards. Underground tanks provide limited direct amenity and biodiversity.

Maintenance

Inspect and clean inlets, sediment controls, outlets and accessible storage areas.

Evidence to check

Choose above-ground and distributed storage where it fits the site. Explain interception and treatment separately, including safe access for repair and replacement.

Check the full drainage requirements and lifetime maintenance arrangements for the proposed system.

Detailed component design: CIRIA C753, chapter 21. Read alongside the current national standards below.

Relevant assessment checks

FE-01No flooding up to 3.3% AEP plus climate changeRequirement

No flooding on any part of the site up to the 3.3% AEP (1 in 30 year) event plus climate change, unless the area is designed to hold or convey water.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Model results for the 3.3% AEP + cc event.
Evidence to look for
Levelled exceedance plan and relevant sections; Event results identifying flooded and protected locations; Outfall, blockage, surcharge and failure assessments
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

FE-02Protect buildings, plant and escape routes up to 1% AEP plus climate changeRequirement

No flooding of buildings (including basements), utility plant susceptible to water, or safe access and escape routes up to the 1% AEP event plus climate change. Manage any system flooding within the development.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Flood volumes and locations for 1% AEP + cc against levels.
Evidence to look for
Levelled exceedance plan and relevant sections; Event results identifying flooded and protected locations; Outfall, blockage, surcharge and failure assessments

FE-03Below-ground storage: suggested modelling approachesRecommendation

Where there is no above-ground storage, Torbay suggests either: 1) 1% AEP + cc with top water level below the incoming pipe invert; or 2) 1% AEP with no flooding, and 3.3% AEP + cc top water level below the incoming pipe invert.

Applies when
Systems with only below-ground storage.
What to check
Top water level against incoming invert.
Evidence to look for
Levelled exceedance plan and relevant sections; Event results identifying flooded and protected locations; Outfall, blockage, surcharge and failure assessments
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

These are suggested modelling approaches. They do not replace the applicable national climate-adjusted performance, flooding and protected-receptor checks in FE-01 and FE-02.

CM-12Design life and loadingRequirement

Show components last the development's design life (or include replacement in the plan) and resist design loads with factors of safety. Underground storage must suit the final surface use.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Product specifications; structural adequacy of crates under parking.
Evidence to look for
Asset-specific maintenance plan and inspection information; Ownership, adoption and usable access arrangements; Construction, phasing, commissioning and structural evidence
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.
