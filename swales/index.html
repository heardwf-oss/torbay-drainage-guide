Where it fits

Sites with suitable levels and enough width for safe side slopes, planting and maintenance.

What to consider

Gradient, check dams where useful, capacity, water quality, erosion, infiltration or lining, crossings and overflow.

Design limits

Steep gradients and concentrated inflows can cause erosion. Underground services and road crossings need coordination.

Maintenance

Manage vegetation, sediment, litter, erosion and inlet or outlet blockages.

Evidence to check

Check national standard 2.7 before claiming interception; the gradient, base material, contributing area and outlet arrangement matter.

Check the full drainage requirements and lifetime maintenance arrangements for the proposed system.

Detailed component design: CIRIA C753, chapter 17. Read alongside the current national standards below.

Relevant assessment checks

ER-09SwalesConditional

Fall under 1:100 with at least 500 mm suitable base: up to 5× the vegetated base area. Unlined, fall under 1:100 and over 1×10⁻⁶ m/s: up to 25×. Steeper than 1:100: no interception unless shown by design. Areas draining within 5 m of an unlined swale outlet do not count unless the swale is flat with a raised outlet.

Applies when
Swales.
What to check
Long-section fall, base material, lining and outlet arrangement.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

This is a compliance presumption for the proposed feature, not a requirement to provide it. Apply the agreement and calculation conditions in ER-05 and national standards 2.6.2–2.7.

WQ-01Base the treatment on a risk assessmentRequirement

Base the management train on a water quality risk assessment matched to the pollution hazard and receiving water sensitivity. Use the Simple Index Approach (C753) or another agreed method.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Hazard level, sensitivity and treatment indices.
Evidence to look for
Pollution-risk and treatment assessmentTrace land use, receptor sensitivity and pollutant risk to the treatment train and its supporting performance evidence.
Gap to check
A separator specification without the site and receiving-water assessment.
