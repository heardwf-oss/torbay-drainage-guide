Where it fits

Roofs, streets, car parks or small paved catchments where a planted feature fits the layout.

What to consider

Inflow, soil layers, storage depth, infiltration or underdrain, lining, planting, treatment and safe overflow.

Design limits

The feature needs suitable soil, a clear drainage route and enough space. Poor inflow details can cause erosion or uneven use.

Maintenance

Remove litter and sediment, maintain planting, inspect inlets and drains, and replace failing soil or plants.

Evidence to check

Show the area drained, the water-quality assessment and how the feature delivers interception. Lined and unlined systems need different evidence.

Check the full drainage requirements and lifetime maintenance arrangements for the proposed system.

Detailed component design: CIRIA C753, chapter 18. Read alongside the current national standards below.

Relevant assessment checks

ER-12Bioretention and rain gardensConditional

Unlined bioretention complies where the impermeable area is up to 5× the vegetated surface receiving runoff (100 m² needs 20 m²).

Applies when
Rain gardens, bioretention, tree pits designed as bioretention.
What to check
Unlined; area ratio.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

This is a compliance presumption for the proposed feature, not a requirement to provide it. Apply the agreement and calculation conditions in ER-05 and national standards 2.6.2–2.7.

AB-03Planting, soils and biosecurityConditional

Where water is near the surface, vegetation and trees should be included as far as reasonably practicable. Species selection shall suit location, temperature, waterlogging and drought. Soil and aggregate specifications shall follow appropriate guidance. Planting operations shall comply with BS 3936-1:1992, BS 3936-10:1990 and BS 8545:2014, as specified in national footnote 32. Plant-selection guidance and sourcing that minimises biosecurity risks are recommendations.

Applies when
Vegetation or amenity features are proposed, with the recommendation to include vegetation applying where water is near the surface.
What to check
Planting schedule, species tolerance, soil and aggregate specification and compliance with the planting standards in national footnote 32.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment; Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings; Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.
