Information to check

A full proposal needs a detailed surface-water drainage scheme, with evidence that the whole system can be built and perform as designed.

Use these headings to check the submission. Record findings against the application reference.

Site and catchment informationLocation, boundary, levels, drainage catchments, existing drains, watercourses, outfalls and constraints.
Flood risk assessmentAssess all relevant flood sources, future climate and safe site operation. Use a proportionate assessment for the proposal.
Runoff destination and agreementsShow how higher-priority destinations have been maximised, the selected outfall, capacity, discharge parameters and rights.
Infiltration tests and groundwater evidenceProvide location- and depth-specific testing, seasonal groundwater evidence and contamination and stability assessments where relevant.
National standards statementExplain how all seven national SuDS standards are met. Record and justify any departure and the agreement sought.
Interception and treatment evidenceIdentify contributing surfaces, first 5 mm interception and the water-quality assessment and treatment train.
Hydraulic design and storageShow existing and proposed rates and volumes, rainfall data, climate allowance, urban creep, event results and critical storage durations.
Drainage layout and levelsIdentify each feature, pipe connection, flow control, outfall, ground level, floor level and available storage.
Safe exceedance and failure routesMap flows beyond the design event, blocked controls, surcharged outfalls and flood-locking; show effects on and off site.
Amenity and biodiversityInclude the biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment produced and agreed at the outset under standard 6.4. Coordinate drainage, landscape and ecology; explain benefits and safety.
Ownership and maintenanceIdentify lifetime ownership, tasks, access, inspections, replacement responsibilities and funding.
Construction details and phased deliveryProvide feature and control details, connecting-network information, construction protection, temporary drainage and commissioning arrangements.
Technical and non-technical summariesProvide calculation inputs and outputs, plus a clear explanation of the design and the conclusions.
Sequential and exception tests where applicableCheck current NPPF F5 and F6, their exemptions and the local validation requirements. Provide the evidence needed for this proposal and stage.
Pollution prevention plan where applicableFor demolition or major development, check the 250 m Lyme Bay and Torbay SAC buffer and nearby watercourses with a hydrological link. Include the qualified-person plan required by Local Validation List 3.12.1.

Keep documents consistent

Use drawing numbers and revisions. Coordinate the drainage information with the layout, levels, landscape and ecology plans. Provide a clear record of changes.
