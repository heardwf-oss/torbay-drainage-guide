Evidence for this topic

Documents to look for

  • Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan
  • Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach
  • Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement

What the evidence should show

  • How runoff from each positively drained surface is retained, used or lost within the development.
  • The actual contributing and receiving areas, lining, levels, gradient and outlet arrangement.
  • Every condition supporting a feature-specific presumption, including relevant infiltration evidence.

Target 2 checks

ER-01Intercept the first 5 mmRequirement

Deliver interception within the development so the first 5 mm of rainfall produces no runoff for at least 80% of summer events (May to October) and 50% of winter events (November to April).

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Interception method for every positively drained surface.
Evidence to look for
Surface-by-surface interception scheduleIdentify each positively drained surface, its receiving feature, the loss or reuse mechanism and the evidence supporting it.
Gap to check
One site-wide assertion that all SuDS provide interception.

ER-03Deemed compliance: harvestingConditional

Optional deemed-compliance route: a development is deemed compliant if rainwater harvesting means no runoff leaves the development for lower-order events, shown by BS EN 16941 calculations. This does not require the feature to be provided.

Applies when
Harvesting serves all relevant areas.
What to check
BS EN 16941 calculations.
Evidence to look for

ER-04Deemed compliance: full infiltration of monthly eventsConditional

Optional deemed-compliance route: a development is deemed compliant if it infiltrates entirely to ground so no runoff leaves for events with a 1 in 1 or greater chance each month, shown by calculations and infiltration tests. A soakaway designed for 1 in 1 or 1 in 10 year events already meets this. This does not require the feature to be provided.

Applies when
Fully infiltrating sites.
What to check
Infiltration calculations and tests.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

ER-08Permeable surfacesConditional

No added area: complies (lined or unlined). Unlined with added impermeable area no greater than the permeable area: complies. Unlined over ground above 1×10⁻⁶ m/s: up to 5× the permeable area may be added (100 m² impermeable needs 20 m² permeable). Lined with added area: does not comply; add downstream interception.

Applies when
Permeable paving.
What to check
Lining, added area ratio and tested rate.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement

ER-09SwalesConditional

Fall under 1:100 with at least 500 mm suitable base: up to 5× the vegetated base area. Unlined, fall under 1:100 and over 1×10⁻⁶ m/s: up to 25×. Steeper than 1:100: no interception unless shown by design. Areas draining within 5 m of an unlined swale outlet do not count unless the swale is flat with a raised outlet.

Applies when
Swales.
What to check
Long-section fall, base material, lining and outlet arrangement.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

This is a compliance presumption for the proposed feature, not a requirement to provide it. Apply the agreement and calculation conditions in ER-05 and national standards 2.6.2–2.7.

ER-11Detention basinsConditional

A flat, unlined vegetated basin base below the outlet, without specific provision routing low flows directly to the outlet, can receive up to five times that base area. Above 1×10⁻⁶ m/s infiltration, the ratio can be up to 25:1. Higher ratios need justification using below-outlet storage, higher infiltration rates and detailed design. Provide upstream sediment control for infiltrating basins.

Applies when
Detention basins.
What to check
Effective below-outlet base area, lining, levels, low-flow routing, tested infiltration and sediment control; calculations for a higher ratio.
Evidence to look for
Basin catchment plan and sectionIdentify effective receiving area, lining, infiltration, low-flow route and levels below the outlet. Test every applicable basin condition.
Gap to check
An area ratio alone, or storage above the outlet used to prove interception.

This is a compliance presumption for the proposed feature, not a requirement to provide it. Apply the agreement and calculation conditions in ER-05 and national standards 2.6.2–2.7.
