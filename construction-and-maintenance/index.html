Evidence for this topic

Documents to look for

  • Asset-specific maintenance plan and inspection information
  • Ownership, adoption and usable access arrangements
  • Construction, phasing, commissioning and structural evidence

What the evidence should show

  • Who maintains each asset and how lifetime performance will be sustained.
  • Practical access, rights, cleaning, replacement and failure response.
  • Protection during construction, hydraulic operation through each phase and suitability for imposed loads.

Maintenance 8 checks

CM-01Management and maintenance planRequirement

Provide a management and maintenance plan that sustains performance against standards 1–6 for the development’s lifetime. Torbay asks for the plan and an inspection checklist. The detailed management and maintenance contents in national standards 7.10–7.11 are recommendations.

Applies when
All applicable development; establish the delivery arrangements at outline and supply detail appropriate to the application stage.
What to check
Responsibilities and funding; asset and land ownership; easements; operating and pollution-incident instructions; establishment and routine maintenance; inspections, sediment and pollution controls and disposal; remedial work, replacement and post-flood rehabilitation.
Evidence to look for
Asset-specific management and maintenance planIdentify each asset, responsible party, lifetime arrangements and practical maintenance. Use the recommended contents in their stated context.
Gap to check
A generic schedule with no asset ownership or delivery arrangements.

CM-02Consider failure scenariosRequirement

Examine the likelihood and consequences of operational failure, including blocked structures and controls and failed pumps. Evaluate both flood risk and water quality impacts and manage the risks. The design should make consequential blockage or clogging apparent from surface inspection.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Flood-risk and water-quality failure assessment, safe operation and the means of detecting failure.
Evidence to look for
Asset-specific maintenance plan and inspection information; Ownership, adoption and usable access arrangements; Construction, phasing, commissioning and structural evidence
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

Construction 3 checks

CM-09Construction information and phasingRequirement

Give the contractor information on protecting and commissioning drainage during construction. For phased developments, provide a phased plan showing flow control in each phase.

Applies when
Full, reserved matters, conditions.
What to check
Construction drainage plan; phasing plan.
Evidence to look for
Construction and phasing informationExplain temporary drainage, protection of infiltration and treatment surfaces, and how each phase remains operational.
Gap to check
Only the final layout shown, with no treatment of construction runoff or incomplete phases.

All development.
What to check
Agreement in principle and a fallback if adoption is refused.
Evidence to look for
Asset-specific maintenance plan and inspection information; Ownership, adoption and usable access arrangements; Construction, phasing, commissioning and structural evidence
