Evidence for this topic

Documents to look for

  • Located investigation and test records
  • Groundwater and geotechnical evidence
  • Feature sections and infiltration calculations

What the evidence should show

  • Tests representative of the proposed feature locations and depths, with a traceable design rate.
  • Feature base and maximum likely groundwater on a common level datum.
  • The adopted safety factor, half-drain performance, stability and pollution assessment.

Testing 2 checks

IG-01Prove infiltration with site testingRequirement

Infiltration depends on suitable ground and adequate tested rates. Geotechnical investigation shall confirm the ground is suitable using recognised methods. Mapping can form an initial evidence base.

Applies when
Infiltration proposed, or evidence needed to assess its feasibility. Mapping can inform whether ground investigation is necessary.
What to check
Adequate site evidence for the hierarchy decision; location-specific investigation and testing where infiltration is relied on.
Evidence to look for
Located investigation and test records; Groundwater and geotechnical evidence; Feature sections and infiltration calculations
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

IG-02Test to BRE 365 at the feature location and depthTorbay criterion

Test in strict accordance with BRE 365 at the location and depth of each feature (or within 20 m where results are similar). Three consecutive tests to near emptying; extrapolation from 25% effective depth is not accepted. Permeable paving tested at 20 m intervals with pits at least 1.5 m × 1 m.

Applies when
All infiltration designs.
What to check
Test locations against the layout; three fills; full drainage.
Evidence to look for
Test plan and test recordsMatch test locations, depths, logs and adopted infiltration rate to the proposed feature. Read the Torbay test criterion alongside the detailed ground evidence.
Gap to check
Unlocated test results or a test depth unrelated to the feature base.

Groundwater 2 checks

IG-03Keep 1 m above maximum groundwaterRequirement

The base of infiltration features shall be at least 1 m above the maximum likely groundwater level. For systems wholly relying on infiltration this 1 m unsaturated zone also protects groundwater.

Applies when
All infiltration.
What to check
Base level against groundwater evidence.
Evidence to look for
Groundwater evidence and feature sectionShow maximum likely groundwater and the proposed base on the same datum, with the separation demonstrated.
Gap to check
Groundwater depth quoted without relating it to the feature base.

IG-04Establish wet-winter groundwater levelsRecommendation

Confirm groundwater levels through investigation during high groundwater periods, using records at or near the site, with enough data to show seasonal levels.

Applies when
Infiltration designs.
What to check
Monitoring period and season. Single dry-season readings are not enough.
Evidence to look for
Located investigation and test records; Groundwater and geotechnical evidence; Feature sections and infiltration calculations
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

There is no Torbay rule for the monitoring period. Devon asks for monitoring over November to May (reference only).

Ground risks 6 checks

IG-05No unacceptable instability, pollution or groundwater floodingRequirement

Infiltration shall not cause unacceptable instability or subsidence (including riverbanks and coastal features), groundwater or surface water pollution, groundwater flooding, or ingress to foul or combined sewers.

Applies when
All infiltration.
What to check
Ground risk assessment. Torbay guide 4.2 lists slopes over 10%, unstable or contaminated ground, services and proximity.
Evidence to look for
Located investigation and test records; Groundwater and geotechnical evidence; Feature sections and infiltration calculations
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

IG-11Assess diffuse infiltration within 3 m of buildingsRequirement

Diffuse near-surface infiltration taking direct rainfall or a very small catchment similar in area to the infiltration surface should not normally pose a structural risk. Such proposals within 3 m of a building shall be assessed and mitigated where required. This reassurance does not extend to large contributing catchments.

Applies when
Permeable surfaces or similar within 3 m of a building.
What to check
Distance, contributing area relative to the infiltration surface, ground conditions, structural risk and any mitigation.
Evidence to look for
Located investigation and test records; Groundwater and geotechnical evidence; Feature sections and infiltration calculations
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

Hydraulic design 4 checks

IG-07Do not rely only on point infiltration below 1×10⁻⁶ m/sRequirement

Point infiltration (for example soakaways) shall not be fully relied on where infiltration rates are below 1×10⁻⁶ m/s.

Applies when
Soakaways and other point features.
What to check
Tested rate. If lower, an additional destination.
Evidence to look for
Located investigation and test records; Groundwater and geotechnical evidence; Feature sections and infiltration calculations
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

IG-08Half-empty within 24 hoursRequirement

Infiltration features shall half-empty their runoff volume within 24 hours for events up to the 3.3% AEP event.

Applies when
All infiltration features.
What to check
Half-drain time in the calculations.
Evidence to look for
Infiltration storage and half-drain calculationIdentify the tested rate, storage geometry and safety factor used to demonstrate the stated half-drain performance.
Gap to check
A storage-volume result without a drain-time check.

IG-09Use the Torbay factor of safetyTorbay criterion

Size infiltration with the RP156 factor of safety. Torbay expects the 'minor damage to external areas or inconvenience' values: 2 for areas under 100 m², 3 for 100 to 1000 m², 5 for over 1000 m². Use 10 where failure would damage buildings or cause major inconvenience.

Applies when
Infiltration designed with the RP156 (CIRIA) method.
What to check
Factor used against drained area and consequence.
Evidence to look for
Calculation input and consequence assessmentTrace the RP156 factor to the drained area and consequence of failure, and check it is applied in the calculation.
Gap to check
A factor stated in the narrative but absent or different in the calculation.
