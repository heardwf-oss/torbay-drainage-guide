FR-01Check the critical drainage area boundarySite and flood riskOfficer guidance

Use the Torbay CDA criteria for conservative screening across Torbay. For a site in Maidencombe, establish the current EA designation and boundary before relying on CDA status in a formal assessment.

Applies when
All development that could affect drainage.
What to check
The site against the current designation map, the applicable local policy and any written confirmation of the 2026 extension. Record the boundary and the basis of the assessment.
Evidence to look for
Dated designation map and recorded assessment basisLocate the application boundary and identify the source version. For Maidencombe, record the published source difference and the confirmation relied on.
Gap to check
A general statement that the whole borough is designated, without addressing the boundary evidence.
Local interpretation

Sources differ. The Local Plan (2015) and SFRA Level 1 (June 2026) say the whole of Torbay is a CDA. The Validation List (November 2024) and the draft SFRA Level 2A (June 2026) say Maidencombe is outside it. The EA national dataset published in September 2026 has a July 2025 geometry revision and does not establish the extension. Whole-Torbay screening is the working interpretation, not confirmation of a new formal boundary. The local Validation List still requires an FRA in Maidencombe.

FR-06Apply the sequential testSite and flood riskConditional

Steer development to the lowest flood risk land where suitable alternative sites are reasonably available. Torbay's Validation List includes a flooding sequential test for new dwellings in Flood Zones 2 and 3. Check the current national exemptions and the applicable local requirements.

Applies when
Development at risk now or in future from any source, unless an exemption in NPPF F5.2 is demonstrated.
What to check
That the sequential test is passed or the precise F5.2 exemption is supported by evidence. The test area should not exceed the anticipated catchment of the development's occupiers or users.
Evidence to look for
Sequential-test evidence or precise exemptionMatch the proposal and FRA to the relevant F5.2 exemption, or demonstrate the sequential test using suitable reasonably available alternatives and the appropriate catchment.
Gap to check
An obsolete change-of-use exemption or an unsupported assertion that the site is exempt.

F5.2 exempts allocated sites tested during plan preparation; sites where an FRA clearly demonstrates that no built development, access or escape routes, land raising or other vulnerable elements are on land at risk now or in future; or surface-water-only risk where layout, design and mitigation meet F7 lifetime safety without increasing risk elsewhere. It also exempts householder development, non-residential extensions with a footprint less than 250 m², and changes of use other than to caravan, camping, chalet, mobile home or park home sites.

Evidence to look for

RD-01Follow the destination hierarchyWhere water goesRequirement

Discharge runoff to the following destinations, to the maximum extent practicable, in this order: 1 collect for non-potable use; 2 infiltrate to ground; 3 discharge to an above-ground surface water body; 4 discharge to a surface water sewer or other piped surface water system; 5 discharge to a combined sewer.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
That each higher destination has been assessed and used as far as practicable.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisal and catchment split plan; Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections; Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

The Local Plan (2015) and the Torbay guide (2022) start the hierarchy at infiltration. The national standards (2025) put reuse first. Reuse first is the current national position in standard 1.2.
