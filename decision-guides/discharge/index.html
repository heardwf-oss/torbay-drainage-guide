Where is the proposed discharge going?

  • Infiltration
  • Watercourse or sewer
  • Tidal water or the sea
  • The destination is not established

Check infiltration performance and overflow

Use the infiltration checks for the proposed system. Identify any positive overflow or alternative discharge route and assess its applicable criteria separately.

  • IG-01 · Prove infiltration with site testing
  • IG-08 · Half-empty within 24 hours
  • IG-09 · Use the Torbay factor of safety
  • RD-17 · Ask for a fallback destination

Assess the receiving-water exception and local context

Read the national exception for tidal or sea discharges, then assess tide-locking, receiving conditions, pollution, local controls and necessary agreements. Do not assume a coastal location creates an unrestricted outfall.

  • RV-08 · Tidal or sea outfalls
  • FE-07 · Surcharged outfalls and flood-locking
  • WQ-08 · Outfall hierarchy
  • RD-11 · Agree discharge to a watercourse in writing

Establish a deliverable destination first

Follow the destination hierarchy and identify the receiving system, route, capacity, rights and required agreements before selecting design discharge criteria.

  • RD-01 · Follow the destination hierarchy
  • RD-02 · Evidence for using a lower destination
  • RD-05 · Secure rights over third-party land
  • RD-11 · Agree discharge to a watercourse in writing
  • RD-12 · Agree sewer connections in writing with capacity

Is the volume discharged to surface waters or sewers in the developed 1% AEP, six-hour event greater than greenfield runoff for the same event?

  • Yes
  • No
  • Not established

Use the increased-volume national branch, then compare local criteria

National clauses 3.18 and 3.19 use the greater of the 50% AEP greenfield rate and 3 l/s/ha for the relevant rate tests. Check the contributing area and baseline, then compare applicable local event-specific greenfield criteria and the Q10 ceiling. Record source differences and any agreement.

  • RV-03 · 50% AEP limit
  • RV-04 · 1% AEP limit: volume test
  • RV-05 · Compare the applicable national and local limits
  • RV-06 · Check proposals relying on the published 1.5 l/s minimum
  • RV-07 · Brownfield sites: check the greenfield expectation

Use the no-increase national branch, then compare local criteria

The national 50% AEP test uses the greater of its greenfield rate and 3 l/s/ha. Clause 3.20 uses the greater of the 1% AEP greenfield rate and 3 l/s/ha for the 1% test. Compare applicable local criteria separately and record the governing basis.

  • RV-03 · 50% AEP limit
  • RV-04 · 1% AEP limit: volume test
  • RV-05 · Compare the applicable national and local limits
  • RV-06 · Check proposals relying on the published 1.5 l/s minimum
  • RV-07 · Brownfield sites: check the greenfield expectation

Obtain the discharged-volume comparison

The 1% AEP national limit cannot be established from peak-rate figures alone. Compare greenfield runoff volume with the developed volume discharged to surface waters or sewers. Distinguish runoff generated from the volume remaining after interception, reuse or infiltration. Check the entire area potentially draining to the system and the correct model inputs.

  • RV-04 · 1% AEP limit: volume test
  • RV-09 · Greenfield runoff method
  • RV-14 · Model the SuDS and pipes together
