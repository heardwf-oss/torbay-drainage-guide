Can this site use infiltration?

Establish the ground evidence before relying on infiltration or moving down the destination hierarchy.

OverviewAssess an applicationRules and guidanceCheck a numberSuDS featuresReview aidsSourcesSearch the guide

This route identifies the checks and evidence to examine. Record the finding against the application; the answers do not grant an approval or create an exemption.

Read all the guidance in this route

What does the initial site evidence show?

  • Infiltration may be feasible
  • A site constraint may rule it out
  • There is not enough evidence

Establish the initial ground evidence

Review mapping, available investigations, geology, groundwater, contamination and stability. Initial mapping can inform whether further investigation is necessary; it is not proof of a detailed infiltration design.

  • IG-01 · Prove infiltration with site testing
  • RD-02 · Evidence for using a lower destination

Is the reason for discounting infiltration supported by site evidence?

  • Yes — the evidence explains the constraint
  • No, or uncertain

Record the constraint and the hierarchy appraisal

Record why infiltration cannot be used, whether any part remains practicable, and the evidence supporting the next destination. Where contamination is the constraint, assess practicable remediation together with the drainage design before discounting a higher destination. Testing is not automatically necessary where adequate initial evidence establishes that it is not needed.

  • RD-02 · Evidence for using a lower destination
  • RD-03 · Maximise each destination in order
  • RD-04 · Contamination is not an automatic reason to move down
  • IG-01 · Prove infiltration with site testing
  • IG-05 · No unacceptable instability, pollution or groundwater flooding

Are detailed tests and ground evidence relevant to the proposed locations and depths?

  • Yes
  • No, or not supplied

Obtain the ground evidence needed for the proposed design

Check the Torbay test method, proposed locations and depths, and relevant seasonal groundwater evidence. Identify stability and pollution risks before accepting a design rate.

  • IG-02 · Test to BRE 365 at the feature location and depth
  • IG-04 · Establish wet-winter groundwater levels
  • IG-05 · No unacceptable instability, pollution or groundwater flooding
  • IG-13 · Groundwater protection and contaminated land

Are groundwater separation, stability and pollution risks demonstrated?

  • Yes
  • No, or uncertain

Resolve the ground constraints

Read the feature sections and ground assessment together. A favourable infiltration rate alone does not establish safe groundwater separation, stability or pollution performance.

  • IG-03 · Keep 1 m above maximum groundwater
  • IG-05 · No unacceptable instability, pollution or groundwater flooding
  • IG-10 · Assess point infiltration within 5 m of structures
  • IG-11 · Assess diffuse infiltration within 3 m of buildings
  • IG-13 · Groundwater protection and contaminated land

Check the complete infiltration design

Verify adopted rate, method, factor of safety and half-drain performance. Check the applicable requirements and recommendations for clogging protection, loading and exceedance. Record the finding against the evidence; this route does not approve a soakaway.

  • IG-07 · Do not rely only on point infiltration below 1×10⁻⁶ m/s
  • IG-08 · Half-empty within 24 hours
  • IG-09 · Use the Torbay factor of safety
  • IG-12 · Protect infiltration surfaces from clogging
  • IG-14 · Structural loading below trafficked surfaces
  • FE-09 · Exceedance routes
