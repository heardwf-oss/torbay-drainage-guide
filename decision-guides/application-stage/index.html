What evidence is needed at this stage?

Use a feasible strategy at outline and trace later detail back to the permission, strategy and conditions.

This route identifies the checks and evidence to examine. Record the finding against the application; the answers do not grant an approval or create an exemption.

Read all the guidance in this route

Which submission are you reviewing?

  • Outline application
  • Full application
  • Reserved matters
  • Discharge of conditions
  • Householder or prior approval

Establish that the strategy can be delivered

Check destination, constraints, land, levels, space, receiving capacity and lifetime delivery. Identify later detail explicitly. A condition should not stand in for evidence that the strategy is feasible.

  • SP-05 · Proportionate evidence by stage
  • RD-01 · Follow the destination hierarchy
  • RD-05 · Secure rights over third-party land

Check the coordinated design package

Trace applicable standards through investigations, calculations, drawings, agreements and maintenance arrangements. Check that revisions and inputs agree across the package.

  • SP-02 · Statement of how the national standards are met
  • SP-04 · Torbay submission list
  • SP-05 · Proportionate evidence by stage
  • RV-14 · Model the SuDS and pipes together
  • CM-01 · Management and maintenance plan

Start with the outline permission and approved strategy

Compare the detailed layout, drainage design and relevant conditions with the established strategy. Investigate changes that affect feasibility, capacity or the approved basis.

  • SP-04 · Torbay submission list
  • SP-05 · Proportionate evidence by stage
  • CM-10 · Build to the approved design

Answer the exact condition

Identify each relevant part of the condition and the drawing, calculation or agreement that satisfies it. Keep the approved strategy and subsequent revisions together.

  • SP-04 · Torbay submission list
  • SP-05 · Proportionate evidence by stage
  • CM-10 · Build to the approved design

Choose proportionate evidence for the actual planning route

Check the Torbay proforma scope, validation requirements and drainage risks. Establish which matters the route permits the council to consider. Application size alone does not remove a substantive requirement.

  • FR-03 · Require a flood risk assessment with a surface water drainage strategy
  • FR-04 · Require a site-specific FRA where NPPF F4 applies
  • SP-05 · Proportionate evidence by stage
