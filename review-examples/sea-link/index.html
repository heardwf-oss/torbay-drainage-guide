02

Interception and ground constraints

Open PDF page 34 · Printed pages 32–33, section 6.5

In the submission
The discussion links interception to feature geometry and ground conditions, including failed tests and shallow groundwater.
Officer’s check
Identify the calculations and feature conditions that demonstrate achieved interception; the narrative alone does not establish performance.
  • ER-01 · Intercept the first 5 mm
  • ER-11 · Detention basins
  • IG-03 · Keep 1 m above maximum groundwater
03

Water quality at the design stage

Open PDF page 44 · Printed page 42, sections 8.1.1–8.1.8

In the submission
The strategy identifies construction pollution risks; treatment depends on final layouts, with proprietary treatment absent from drawings at this stage.
Officer’s check
Track the outstanding treatment details into the drawings and risk assessment.
  • WQ-01 · Base the treatment on a risk assessment
  • WQ-02 · Provide a management train
  • CM-09 · Construction information and phasing
04

A maintenance table and its limits

Open PDF page 53 · Printed pages 51–52, section 11.2 and table 11.1

In the submission
Regular, occasional and remedial tasks are separated. The maintainer is only a likely operator, and the asset plan remains to be developed.
Officer’s check
Check who will deliver the plan, with practical access and lifetime arrangements.
  • CM-01 · Management and maintenance plan
  • CM-03 · Access for maintenance
  • CM-14 · Adoption routes
