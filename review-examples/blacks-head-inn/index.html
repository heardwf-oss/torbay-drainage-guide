Smaller renovation and extension · drainage strategy, Issue 3, December 2025

Cherwell application 25/03340/F was permitted on 30 September 2026, subject to conditions; official record checked 3 October 2026. Condition 2 includes this Drainage Strategy Issue 3. This example does not establish Torbay compliance.

01

Ground evidence before design choices

Open PDF page 8 · Printed page 4, sections 2.9–2.10

In the submission
The report links infiltration feasibility to October 2025 testing.
Officer’s check
Match the investigation to the actual feature locations, depths, groundwater and adopted calculation rate.
  • IG-01 · Prove infiltration with site testing
  • IG-02 · Test to BRE 365 at the feature location and depth
  • IG-03 · Keep 1 m above maximum groundwater
02

The destination appraisal

Open PDF page 9 · Printed page 5, sections 3.2–3.4 and table 3.1

In the submission
Five destinations are considered, with proposed and discounted options recorded.
Officer’s check
Check the evidence for each conclusion and whether higher destinations are used as far as practicable.
  • RD-01 · Follow the destination hierarchy
  • RD-02 · Evidence for using a lower destination
  • RD-03 · Maximise each destination in order
03

Catchments and calculations

Open PDF page 10 · Printed page 6, sections 3.5–3.11

In the submission
Catchments, test rates, sizing, rainfall, factor of safety and exceedance are connected in the narrative.
Officer’s check
Reconcile those inputs with the test records, calculation output and level drawings.
  • IG-09 · Use the Torbay factor of safety
  • RV-10 · Climate change: upper end allowance
  • RV-14 · Model the SuDS and pipes together
  • FE-09 · Exceedance routes
04

Interception, treatment and upkeep

Open PDF page 11 · Printed page 7, sections 3.12–3.16; maintenance table on printed page 9

In the submission
The report states interception, pollution and maintenance arrangements.
Officer’s check
Examine the underlying interception calculation, land-use hazard and practical maintenance; a confident conclusion is not the evidence.
  • ER-01 · Intercept the first 5 mm
  • WQ-01 · Base the treatment on a risk assessment
  • CM-01 · Management and maintenance plan

Read the authority’s response too

The 27 May 2026 drainage response gives an in-principle no objection with recommendations on surface SuDS, separation, lifetime maintenance and local standards. Compare that qualified response with the applicant’s conclusions.
