Follow the evidence through the reviews

  1. 12 May 2026 · first review

    The reviewer requests targeted evidence: rates, area, land use, route feasibility, ownership, headwall details and maintenance. It distinguishes proportionate evidence from the need for full printouts.

    Open this document (PDF)

  2. 9 June 2026 · applicant’s response

    The response points to revised calculations, an area plan, ownership information, a watercourse survey and maintenance responsibility.

    Open this document (PDF)

  3. 22 June 2026 · second review

    Several entries improve, but the reviewer still requires clarification of whether the proposed headwall and apron adequately reduce outfall velocities and whether the sewage treatment plant has sufficient capacity. The latter is a foul-drainage issue, separate from the surface-water checks.

    Open this document (PDF)

The review lesson

A revised submission can answer many questions while material issues remain unresolved. Keep each original request, revised evidence and outstanding finding traceable.
