Look for

  • Greenfield calculation and contributing-area schedule
  • Coordinated hydraulic model and input schedule
  • Event results, volume comparison and non-technical summary

The evidence should demonstrate

  • Which national branch applies, using developed volume discharged to surface waters or sewers compared with greenfield runoff for the same 1% AEP, six-hour event.
  • Applicable local limits shown separately, with their geographical and policy basis.
  • Lifetime, climate allowance, urban creep, rainfall and duration assumptions reconciled with the drawings.

Common gaps

  • One peak-flow result without the required volume comparison.
  • Existing brownfield discharge used as the greenfield baseline.
  • A minimum discharge rate applied automatically.
  • Model areas, levels or storage that do not match the drawings.

Record the finding

Identify the document, revision, drawing or page. Explain which part of the check the evidence supports and what remains unresolved. A document title or completed template does not establish that its contents meet the criterion.
