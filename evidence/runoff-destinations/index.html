Look for

  • Destination appraisal and catchment split plan
  • Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections
  • Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights

The evidence should demonstrate

  • How each higher destination is used as far as practicable and why a lower destination is needed.
  • Where each area drains and whether a gravity connection is deliverable.
  • The proposed connection, receiving capacity, rates, volumes and necessary lifetime rights.

Common gaps

  • A nearby sewer shown on a map without connection or capacity evidence.
  • Higher destinations dismissed as “too expensive” or “unsuitable” without evidence.
  • An acknowledgement of an enquiry presented as a discharge agreement.

Record the finding

Identify the document, revision, drawing or page. Explain which part of the check the evidence supports and what remains unresolved. A document title or completed template does not establish that its contents meet the criterion.
