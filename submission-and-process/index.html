What the evidence should show

  • A traceable link from each applicable standard to the supporting evidence.
  • A feasible outline strategy and the appropriate detail at later stages.
  • Consistent revisions, clearly identified departures and separate consent routes.

Policy basis 3 checks

SP-01SuDS for all development that could affect drainageRequirement

Development that could affect drainage on or around the site should include SuDS proportionate to its nature and scale, designed to the national standards, with acceptable operation for its lifetime. Major development takes account of LLFA advice.

Applies when
All development that could affect drainage, not only major.
What to check
Proportionate SuDS for the scale.
Evidence to look for
Standards-compliance statement and indexed evidence register; Coordinated strategy, drawings, investigations and calculations; Condition-response and consent schedules where relevant
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

Expect: percolation tests and geotechnical report; one hydraulic model of SuDS and pipes; layout drawings (cover and invert levels, pipe sizes, lengths, gradients, gullies, numbering, catchments); flow control details; SuDS details; construction drawings; maintenance plan and inspection checklist; exceedance and blockage plan; connection permissions; evidence the hierarchy was followed.

SP-10DeparturesOfficer guidance

A national “shall” requirement needs an agreed departure to vary it; a “should” recommendation needs appropriate justification for an alternative. Where constraints prevent full compliance, maximise delivery and develop the justification in consultation with the approving body. A departure cannot override a prohibiting regulatory control. “Must” denotes a legislative or statutory obligation.

Applies when
Any non-compliance.
What to check
The precise requirement, constraints, maximised delivery, reason, agreement and any overriding regulatory control.
Evidence to look for
Standards-compliance statement and indexed evidence register; Coordinated strategy, drawings, investigations and calculations; Condition-response and consent schedules where relevant
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.
