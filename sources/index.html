Using local and national documents together

The national standards set the national design framework. More stringent local runoff controls take precedence where supported by local evidence. Read each local criterion in its geographical and policy context. Devon guidance is a design reference; Devon-specific thresholds are not Torbay rules.

The adopted Torbay Local Plan and the emerging plan are different documents. Draft evidence and consultation proposals do not acquire adopted status through this guide.

Read the local criteria and source differences.
