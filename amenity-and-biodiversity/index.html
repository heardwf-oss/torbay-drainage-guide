Evidence for this topic

Documents to look for

  • Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment
  • Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings
  • Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment

What the evidence should show

  • How existing habitats, hydrology and site character informed the design.
  • Mitigation, habitat connectivity, safe use and planting suited to the water conditions.
  • The required final biodiversity evidence, with BNG addressed where applicable.
  • Landscape context, public appreciation, accessible paths and usable space, with recreation where reasonably practicable; match the evidence to the proposal.

Amenity 5 checks

AB-01Multifunctional designRequirement

Adopt a SuDS approach that maximises amenity and demonstrates multifunctional water management. Keeping runoff at or near the surface is recommended. For surface features, high visual quality should have equal importance to functionality.

Applies when
All applicable development.
What to check
Multifunctional design evidence, landscape integration and the reason for any below-ground solution.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment; Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings; Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

AB-02Follow local landscape guidanceRequirement

Select and design features with regard to topography, existing flow routes, geodiversity, character, habitats, cultural and historical associations, infiltration and discharge potential. Inform the design by relevant LPA landscape guidance, character assessments, green infrastructure strategies, design policies and codes.

Applies when
All applicable development.
What to check
Site assessment and relevant Torbay landscape, green infrastructure and design documents.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment; Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings; Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

AB-03Planting, soils and biosecurityConditional

Where water is near the surface, vegetation and trees should be included as far as reasonably practicable. Species selection shall suit location, temperature, waterlogging and drought. Soil and aggregate specifications shall follow appropriate guidance. Planting operations shall comply with BS 3936-1:1992, BS 3936-10:1990 and BS 8545:2014, as specified in national footnote 32. Plant-selection guidance and sourcing that minimises biosecurity risks are recommendations.

Applies when
Vegetation or amenity features are proposed, with the recommendation to include vegetation applying where water is near the surface.
What to check
Planting schedule, species tolerance, soil and aggregate specification and compliance with the planting standards in national footnote 32.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment; Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings; Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

AB-04Public appreciation and recreationRequirement

Convey or attenuate runoff in features that maximise public appreciation. Where proposals form part of a streetscape or public space, include leisure and recreation where reasonably practicable.

Applies when
All applicable development; the recreation requirement applies to streetscapes and public spaces.
What to check
Access, paths and usable space.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment; Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings; Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment
Gap to check

AB-05Safety by designRequirement

Apply good-practice principles to assess and manage risk in all cases. Shallow slopes, benching and margins are recommended for attenuation features. In community spaces, signage should explain function, public safety risks, benefits and authority contact details.

Applies when
All applicable development; apply the recommendations for attenuation features and community spaces where relevant.
What to check
Risk assessment and management, safe access, depths, side slopes and edges; signage and interpretation where appropriate.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment; Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings; Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

Biodiversity 2 checks

AB-06Biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment at the outsetRequirement

Produce and agree a biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment at the start of design. It covers existing habitats and surveys, sensitivity, scale, local strategies (LNRS, RBMP) and BNG integration.

Applies when
All development, proportionate to scale and sensitivity.
What to check
An agreed assessment at the outset: surveys and baseline habitats; site and wider catchment effects; hydrological changes affecting habitats; scale and sensitivity; local strategies; impacts, mitigation and opportunities; biodiversity net gain integration where applicable.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessmentIdentify baseline habitats and hydrology, effects, opportunities, mitigation and the required agreement with the approving body.
Gap to check
A general landscaping statement produced after drainage choices have already been fixed.

AB-07Show the final design adds biodiversity valueRequirement

Provide evidence that the design follows the biodiversity mitigation hierarchy and relevant designated-site requirements, delivers resilient and self-sustaining habitats and corridors, supports connectivity, considers invasive species and integrates with standards 1–5. Where contributing to biodiversity net gain, local nature recovery or nutrient credits, demonstrate current best practice.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Evidence addressing every item in national standard 6.7, including ecology and drainage interactions, wider-catchment impacts, habitat connectivity and the required mitigation.
Evidence to look for
Early biodiversity risk and opportunity assessment; Coordinated ecology, landscape, planting and drainage drawings; Final biodiversity evidence and safety assessment
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.
