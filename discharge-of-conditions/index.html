Information to check

The decision notice determines which information is needed. Check each requirement in the drainage condition and provide evidence that addresses it.

Use these headings to check the submission. Record findings against the application reference.

Exact condition and requested detailsQuote the drainage condition, identify the application reference and list each requirement it contains.
Approved strategy and relevant drawingsShow how the details comply with the permission and identify revisions clearly.
Detailed hydraulic calculationsInclude the SuDS and connecting network, controls, storage, rainfall inputs and event results required by the condition.
Infiltration and groundwater evidenceInclude the testing and ground-risk evidence needed to support the proposed infiltration system.
Discharge agreement and permissionsProvide the agreed outfall, capacity, rights and connection information required for the design.
Detailed design and exceedance plansShow construction details, levels, flow routes, blockage and surcharge checks, and the impacts on surrounding land.
Lifetime maintenance and inspection planProvide responsibilities, tasks, access, funding and replacement arrangements for the entire system.
Construction runoff and sediment controlsShow temporary drainage, phasing and protection against contamination or damage during the works.
Standards statement and summariesExplain standards compliance and provide the technical and plain-language evidence relevant to the condition.
Sequential and exception tests where applicableCheck current NPPF F5 and F6, their exemptions and the local validation requirements. Provide the evidence needed for this proposal and stage.
Pollution prevention plan where applicableFor demolition or major development, check the 250 m Lyme Bay and Torbay SAC buffer and nearby watercourses with a hydrological link. Include the qualified-person plan required by Local Validation List 3.12.1.

Keep documents consistent

Use drawing numbers and revisions. Coordinate the drainage information with the layout, levels, landscape and ecology plans. Provide a clear record of changes.
