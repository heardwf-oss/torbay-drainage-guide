Evidence for this topic

Documents to look for

  • Land-use and receiving-water risk assessment
  • Treatment-train plan and supporting performance evidence
  • Spill containment, isolation and maintenance details

What the evidence should show

  • The pollution hazard, receptor sensitivity and treatment appropriate to each contributing area.
  • How the successive measures manage pollutants over the design life.
  • How a spill can be isolated, removed and the system restored; infiltration risks assessed separately.

Risk and treatment 7 checks

WQ-01Base the treatment on a risk assessmentRequirement

Base the management train on a water quality risk assessment matched to the pollution hazard and receiving water sensitivity. Use the Simple Index Approach (C753) or another agreed method.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Hazard level, sensitivity and treatment indices.
Evidence to look for
Pollution-risk and treatment assessmentTrace land use, receptor sensitivity and pollutant risk to the treatment train and its supporting performance evidence.
Gap to check
A separator specification without the site and receiving-water assessment.

WQ-03Higher-risk land usesConditional

Higher-risk uses may need proprietary treatment within the management train. Its evidence shall demonstrate necessary treatment throughout the development’s lifetime with appropriate maintenance. Very high-risk runoff requires discrete storage or treatment: where possible, discharge to a foul sewer under consent or use licensed off-site disposal. Environmental discharge is likely to require an EA permit.

Applies when
Commercial yards, lorry parks, fuel or chemical handling, industrial sites.
What to check
Pollution hazard, lifetime treatment evidence, maintenance, discrete facilities where needed, sewer consent or disposal route and any permit.
Evidence to look for
Land-use and receiving-water risk assessment; Treatment-train plan and supporting performance evidence; Spill containment, isolation and maintenance details
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

WQ-10Pollution prevention plan near the SACTorbay criterion

Demolition or major development within 250 m of the Lyme Bay and Torbay SAC, or near a watercourse with a hydrological link to it, needs a pollution prevention plan by a suitably qualified person covering soil and sediment runoff.
