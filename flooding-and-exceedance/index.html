Evidence for this topic

Documents to look for

  • Levelled exceedance plan and relevant sections
  • Event results identifying flooded and protected locations
  • Outfall, blockage, surcharge and failure assessments

What the evidence should show

  • Where water moves when capacity is exceeded and the consequences at site boundaries.
  • The relationship between water levels, buildings, vulnerable plant and escape routes.
  • The effects of incoming runoff, submerged outfalls, blockage and justified freeboard.

Design events 6 checks

FE-01No flooding up to 3.3% AEP plus climate changeRequirement

No flooding on any part of the site up to the 3.3% AEP (1 in 30 year) event plus climate change, unless the area is designed to hold or convey water.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Model results for the 3.3% AEP + cc event.
Evidence to look for
Levelled exceedance plan and relevant sections; Event results identifying flooded and protected locations; Outfall, blockage, surcharge and failure assessments
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

FE-03Below-ground storage: suggested modelling approachesRecommendation

Where there is no above-ground storage, Torbay suggests either: 1) 1% AEP + cc with top water level below the incoming pipe invert; or 2) 1% AEP with no flooding, and 3.3% AEP + cc top water level below the incoming pipe invert.

Applies when
Systems with only below-ground storage.
What to check
Top water level against incoming invert.
Evidence to look for
Levelled exceedance plan and relevant sections; Event results identifying flooded and protected locations; Outfall, blockage, surcharge and failure assessments
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

These are suggested modelling approaches. They do not replace the applicable national climate-adjusted performance, flooding and protected-receptor checks in FE-01 and FE-02.

Off-site flows 2 checks

FE-06Flows from adjacent landRequirement

Account for runoff from adjacent sites onto the development. Convey it safely; no attenuation needed unless it interacts with the system. Off-site flooding up to 1% AEP should be managed or safely routed without increasing downstream risk.

Applies when
Sites receiving overland flow.
What to check
Upslope catchment and routing plan.
Evidence to look for
Levelled exceedance plan and relevant sections; Event results identifying flooded and protected locations; Outfall, blockage, surcharge and failure assessments

FE-07Surcharged outfalls and flood-lockingRequirement

Analyse the effect of a surcharged outfall and flood-locking (watercourse, sea, estuary or groundwater levels), adding storage if needed.

Applies when
Outfalls that may be submerged or surcharged.
What to check
Model run with surcharged outfall.
Evidence to look for
Outfall boundary and surcharge assessmentCompare relevant normal and surcharged conditions, showing changes to stored volume, water levels and protected locations.
Gap to check
A free-discharge model where the receiving system could surcharge or flood-lock.

Exceedance and blockage 2 checks

FE-09Exceedance routesRequirement

Assess events beyond 1% AEP and show exceedance routes on a plan, with no adverse risk to the development or elsewhere.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
Exceedance plan with levels.
Evidence to look for
Levelled exceedance planFollow each route past buildings and access routes to its destination. Check incoming flows and boundary consequences.
Gap to check
Arrows showing direction but no levels, destination or effects on neighbouring land.
