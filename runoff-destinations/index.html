Evidence for this topic

Documents to look for

  • Destination appraisal and catchment split plan
  • Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections
  • Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights

What the evidence should show

  • How each higher destination is used as far as practicable and why a lower destination is needed.
  • Where each area drains and whether a gravity connection is deliverable.
  • The proposed connection, receiving capacity, rates, volumes and necessary lifetime rights.

The hierarchy 4 checks

RD-01Follow the destination hierarchyRequirement

Discharge runoff to the following destinations, to the maximum extent practicable, in this order: 1 collect for non-potable use; 2 infiltrate to ground; 3 discharge to an above-ground surface water body; 4 discharge to a surface water sewer or other piped surface water system; 5 discharge to a combined sewer.

Applies when
All development.
What to check
That each higher destination has been assessed and used as far as practicable.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisal and catchment split plan; Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections; Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

The Local Plan (2015) and the Torbay guide (2022) start the hierarchy at infiltration. The national standards (2025) put reuse first. Reuse first is the current national position in standard 1.2.

RD-02Evidence for using a lower destinationRequirement

Evidence shall show all higher priority destinations have been used to the maximum extent practicable. Higher cost alone is not a reason to use a lower destination.

Applies when
Any use of destinations 2 to 5.
What to check
Site evidence (tests, levels, distances), not assertion or cost alone.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisalFor each higher destination, identify the site evidence, practicable use and reason any remaining runoff needs a lower destination.
Gap to check
A conclusion that infiltration is unsuitable without the supporting assessment.

RD-05Secure rights over third-party landRequirement

Where discharge needs third-party land, provide evidence of easements for the lifetime of the development.

Applies when
Outfalls or routes across land not owned by the applicant.
What to check
Easement or agreement evidence. Where rights cannot be obtained, the council may have powers under FWMA 2010 Schedule 2 paragraph 29(1).
Evidence to look for
Land-rights evidence and route planMatch the proposed connection and access route to the land affected and the rights needed for construction, use and maintenance.
Gap to check
A drainage line crossing third-party land with no evidence of the necessary rights.

Reuse 2 checks

RD-09Consider rainwater harvestingConditional

Rainwater harvesting shall be considered where there is a non-potable demand with enough catchment, a need for landscape irrigation, or the area is seriously water stressed.

Applies when
Any of the three triggers applies. Examples include industrial, commercial, educational, public sector, residential and multiple-occupancy buildings.
What to check
A reasoned demand assessment. If harvesting is not used, the reason.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisal and catchment split plan; Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections; Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

RD-12Agree sewer connections in writing with capacityConditional

Discharge to a surface water sewer, other piped drainage or combined sewer shall be agreed in writing with the relevant risk management authorities and the downstream owner if different. Include rates, volumes, connection location and confirmation of capacity.

RD-13Combined sewer: control to greenfield and assess pollutionConditional

A combined sewer is the last destination in the hierarchy. ER2 requires greenfield discharge rates. Assess pollution from surcharge or flooding of the receiving combined sewer and provide mitigation demonstrating that pollution risk is not increased. Where capacity is insufficient, the developer should work with the undertaker to fund capacity.

Applies when
Destination 5.
What to check
Hierarchy evidence, greenfield rate, receiving sewer capacity, pollution assessment and effective mitigation.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisal and catchment split plan; Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections; Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

Pumping 2 checks

RD-15Pumping by exception onlyConditional

Pumping shall be by exception and agreed with the approving body. Assess pumping against a lower destination reachable by gravity, including cost, failure risk, pollution and flooding. Pump only the parts that cannot drain by gravity.

Applies when
Any pumped surface water.
What to check
The gravity alternative, the area pumped and the failure assessment.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisal and catchment split plan; Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections; Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.
