National SuDS strategy templates

The Environment Agency, CIRIA, the Association of SuDS Authorities and Arup developed templates covering the seven national standards. The Chief Planner’s August 2026 newsletter announced their availability and encouraged local authorities to adopt them.

Use the appropriate template alongside Torbay’s validation requirements. The templates are national submission resources; their publication does not establish local adoption.

  • Major development strategy template (Word)
  • Non-major development strategy template (Word)

Read the scope and instructions in the selected template. The non-major template excludes its defined “minor development” category: work to an existing dwellinghouse or its curtilage, non-domestic extensions of no more than 250 m², and alterations that do not increase building size. This is a template-selection distinction; it does not exempt the proposal from drainage policy or Torbay submission requirements.

Published example: Sea Link, Kent

National Grid · Kent Drainage Strategy · Revision B · March 2026.

A published applicant strategy that explicitly uses the June 2025 national SuDS standards. It provides an example of how evidence, calculations and the drainage narrative can be organised for a major infrastructure proposal.

Torbay forms and design references

Use the Torbay flood risk assessment forms for the applicable submission route. For component design, use the SuDS Manual (C753) alongside the newer national standards.
