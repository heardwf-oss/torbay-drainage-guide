Householder applications

Torbay’s validation list requires a flood risk assessment including a surface water drainage strategy. For most householder proposals in Flood Zone 1, use the council’s short flood risk assessment proforma where appropriate. Check the form’s conditions and the actual flood and drainage risks.

Prior approval and change of use

Identify the precise permitted-development or application route and the matters that can be considered. The validation list includes flood risk information for relevant prior approvals. Establish whether the proposal alters impermeable area, discharge, drainage operation or flood vulnerability.

Keep the evidence proportionate

Check the site, destination, infiltration, receiving system, flood routes and maintenance as relevant. A smaller application does not automatically remove a substantive requirement. Record the reason where a technical check does not apply.
