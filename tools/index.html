Compare runoff rates

Use the entire development area that could drain to the proposed system in an extreme event, including permeable and impermeable surfaces. Enter its greenfield rates in litres per second, without climate change. The baseline greenfield volume also excludes climate change. For the volume test, compare the developed volume discharged to surface waters or sewers with greenfield runoff for the same event. Identify the volume remaining after interception, reuse or infiltration; generated runoff alone is not the comparison. Do not enter rates per hectare.

National standards 3.18–3.20 apply the greater of the relevant greenfield rate and 3 l/s/ha. The comparison also shows the Torbay CDA criterion separately. It applies no automatic 1.5 l/s minimum and no brownfield relaxation. The national rate clauses do not prescribe a separate 3.3% AEP limit. The Torbay CDA comparison shows its applicable Q10 ceiling; the same event also checks on-site flooding.

South Devon rainfall allowances

National SuDS standard 3.8 requires the upper-end allowance, using the development lifetime and latest EA data. Torbay lies in the South Devon management catchment.

Upper-end peak rainfall allowances for drainage design
EpochPeriod3.3% AEP event1% AEP event

2070s2061–212540%50%

Record the development lifetime and selected epoch. Residential development should be assessed for a minimum 100-year lifetime. Where the lifetime extends beyond the published epoch, document how that period has been treated and establish the appropriate approach with the approving body.

General flood risk assessment guidance also contains central allowances for specified lifetimes. Those values do not replace the upper-end requirement in SuDS standard 3.8. Torbay’s September 2022 guide states 50%; where a shorter-lifetime national value is lower, record and agree the treatment of the published local criterion.
