Where it fits

Roofs with a dependable non-potable demand, such as toilet flushing or irrigation.

What to consider

The contributing area, rainfall, demand profile, storage, water quality, controls, overflow and safe separation from drinking water.

Design limits

A reliable demand is needed to empty storage between events. A tank that stays full cannot be assumed to provide storm storage.

Maintenance

Inspect filters, controls, tanks, pumps and overflow arrangements.

Evidence to check

Use a performance calculation appropriate to the design. Where claiming interception, check national standard 2; seasonal garden use alone does not demonstrate compliance.

Check the full drainage requirements and lifetime maintenance arrangements for the proposed system.

Detailed component design: CIRIA C753, chapter 11. Read alongside the current national standards below.

Relevant assessment checks

RD-09Consider rainwater harvestingConditional

Rainwater harvesting shall be considered where there is a non-potable demand with enough catchment, a need for landscape irrigation, or the area is seriously water stressed.

Applies when
Any of the three triggers applies. Examples include industrial, commercial, educational, public sector, residential and multiple-occupancy buildings.
What to check
A reasoned demand assessment. If harvesting is not used, the reason.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisal and catchment split plan; Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections; Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

RD-10Design rainwater harvesting for its intended useConditional

Harvesting used to manage large rainfall events shall be designed to BS EN 16941. Harvesting design shall follow best practice and demonstrate effectiveness against a rainfall time series. Communal systems outside the curtilage shall consider public health and security.

Applies when
Rainwater harvesting is proposed; apply the large-event and communal-system conditions where relevant.
What to check
Rainfall time series, demand profile, applicable design standard and public health and security assessment.
Evidence to look for
Destination appraisal and catchment split plan; Ground evidence, levels and drainage long sections; Written discharge agreements and relevant land rights
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

ER-07Rainwater harvesting and water buttsConditional

Surfaces draining to BS EN 16941 harvesting based on regular daily non-potable demand comply. Water butts limited to garden use in dry periods do not.

Applies when
Harvesting or water butts.
What to check
Daily demand basis; water butts not counted unless designed with calculations.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

This is a compliance presumption for the proposed feature, not a requirement to provide it. Apply the agreement and calculation conditions in ER-05 and national standards 2.6.2–2.7.
