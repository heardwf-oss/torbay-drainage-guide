Where it fits

Paths, parking and other paved areas where structural loading and maintenance are compatible.

What to consider

Surface type, loads, sub-base, infiltration or lining, groundwater, additional contributing area, controls and overflow.

Design limits

Sediment and construction traffic can clog or damage the surface. Ground and vehicle use can require lining or additional treatment.

Maintenance

Inspect the surface, clean it using the appropriate method, and maintain outlets and access points.

Evidence to check

State whether the design is infiltrating, partially infiltrating or lined. Check standard 2.7 limits before claiming interception from adjacent paved areas.

Check the full drainage requirements and lifetime maintenance arrangements for the proposed system.

Detailed component design: CIRIA C753, chapter 20. Read alongside the current national standards below.

Relevant assessment checks

ER-08Permeable surfacesConditional

No added area: complies (lined or unlined). Unlined with added impermeable area no greater than the permeable area: complies. Unlined over ground above 1×10⁻⁶ m/s: up to 5× the permeable area may be added (100 m² impermeable needs 20 m² permeable). Lined with added area: does not comply; add downstream interception.

Applies when
Permeable paving.
What to check
Lining, added area ratio and tested rate.
Evidence to look for
Positively drained surface schedule and catchment plan; Interception calculations or evidence for the applicable simplified approach; Feature sections, area schedules and any required agreement
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.

This is a compliance presumption for the proposed feature, not a requirement to provide it. Apply the agreement and calculation conditions in ER-05 and national standards 2.6.2–2.7.

IG-11Assess diffuse infiltration within 3 m of buildingsRequirement

Diffuse near-surface infiltration taking direct rainfall or a very small catchment similar in area to the infiltration surface should not normally pose a structural risk. Such proposals within 3 m of a building shall be assessed and mitigated where required. This reassurance does not extend to large contributing catchments.

Applies when
Permeable surfaces or similar within 3 m of a building.
What to check
Distance, contributing area relative to the infiltration surface, ground conditions, structural risk and any mitigation.
Evidence to look for
Located investigation and test records; Groundwater and geotechnical evidence; Feature sections and infiltration calculations
Gap to check
The conclusion cannot be traced to the submitted design or supporting evidence.
